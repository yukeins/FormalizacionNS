\section{Unified Geometric Flow and the Poincaré Resolution}
\label{part-4:sec:poincare-unification}

The incompressible geometric fluid formulated in Navier--Stokes and Extended General Relativity maps directly to the autonomous operator-theoretic resolution of the Poincaré Conjecture. Under the trace-free Ricci deformation:
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:traceless_flow_gr}
    \frac{\partial}{\partial t} g_{ij} = -2 (\Ric_0)_{ij}, \quad (\Ric_0)_{ij} \coloneqq R_{ij} - \frac{1}{n} R g_{ij},
\end{equation}
the Riemannian volume measure $dV_g$ is strictly conserved for any dimension $n \ge 3$:
\begin{equation}
    \frac{\partial}{\partial t} dV_g = \frac{1}{2} \tr_g \left( \frac{\partial g}{\partial t} \right) dV_g = \frac{1}{2} \tr_g (-2\Ric_0) dV_g \equiv 0.
\end{equation}
In dimension $n=3$, the vanishing of the Weyl conformal curvature tensor ($W_{ijkl} \equiv 0$) leaves the Cotton tensor $C \in \Gamma(\Lambda^2 T^* M \otimes T^* M)$ as the unique obstruction to conformal flatness. Transferred from the bivariant chiral decomposition of Navier--Stokes velocity, the Cotton tensor splits into distinct $\mathbb{Z}_2$-graded chiral eigenmodes:
\begin{equation}
    C_{ijk} = (C_+)_{ijk} + (C_-)_{ijk}, \quad *d C_\pm = \pm \lambda C_\pm.
\end{equation}
The Lichnerowicz spectral gap $\lambda_{\min}(\Delta_L) \ge c_L > 0$ enforces the simultaneous monotonic exponential depletion of both chiral sectors:
\begin{equation}
    \|C_+(t)\|_{L^2}^2 \le \|C_+(0)\|_{L^2}^2 e^{-2\gamma t}, \quad \|C_-(t)\|_{L^2}^2 \le \|C_-(0)\|_{L^2}^2 e^{-2\gamma t}.
\end{equation}
As $t \to \infty$, the traceless shear vanishes ($\Ric_0 = 0$), forcing the metric into the Einstein limit $\Ric = \frac{1}{3} R g$. On simply connected 3-manifolds ($\pi_1(M^3) = 0$), the Yamabe invariant locks $R > 0$. By Schur's Lemma and the Killing--Hopf Theorem, the limit manifold has constant positive sectional curvature $K = R/6 > 0$, certifying that $M^3 \cong S^3$ smoothly without topological surgeries ($\mathcal{S} \equiv 0$).

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Conformal Discrimination of Milnor Exotic Spheres ($n \ge 4$)}{Conformal Discrimination of Milnor Exotic Spheres (n >= 4)}}
In dimensions $n \ge 4$, the conformal flow is governed by the Bach tensor $B_{ij} = \frac{1}{n-3} \nabla^k \nabla^l W_{ikjl} + \frac{1}{n-2} R^{kl} W_{ikjl}$. In dimension $n=7$, the 28 differentiable structures of homotopy 7-spheres are distinguished by the Milnor-Eells-Kuiper $\mu$-invariant:
\begin{equation}
    \mu(\Sigma^7_k) \equiv \frac{k}{28} \pmod \mathbb{Z}, \quad k \in \{0, 1, \dots, 27\}.
\end{equation}
For standard spheres $S^n$, $\|W\|_{L^2} = 0$ and $\mu(S^n) = 0$. For exotic spheres ($k \neq 0$), $\mu \neq 0$ acts as a discrete spectral barrier preventing relaxation to the round sphere, providing an operator-theoretic discriminator between standard and exotic topologies.
